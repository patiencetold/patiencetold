Services

From the first idea to the final file.

Patience Told is a creative company in Montréal. We work with artists, brands and other creatives on music videos and visuals, and we handle direction, videography, production and design under one roof.

What we do

01

Direction

The idea, and how it should look and feel on screen.

  • Concept and treatment
  • Creative direction
  • Shot planning
02

Videography

  • Content for brands and businesses

Production

  • Planning and scheduling
  • Running the shoot day
  • Edit and final delivery

1. Scope

This policy applies to the website patiencetold.com and to messages you send us through it. The site is also the portfolio of Karim Elnaggar, director and editor. It doesn't cover YouTube, Instagram or any other site we link to or embed; those services have their own policies.

Work we do for clients is also covered by the written agreement for that project.

2. Who is responsible

Patience Told is a creative company based in Montréal, Québec, Canada. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Patience Told.

The person responsible for the protection of personal information is Karim Elnaggar, Director. You can reach him at karim@patiencetold.com.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you send the contact form, we receive:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • the industry and type of project you select
  • the content of your message, including anything you choose to tell us about yourself or your project

Information collected automatically

Our web host keeps standard server logs when you visit: IP address, date and time, the page requested, and the browser and device type. We use these only for security and to keep the site running.

We don't use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking tools, and we don't build profiles of visitors.

Information from other sources

We don't buy or collect personal information about you from third parties.

4. Why we use it

We use personal information only to:

  • reply to your message
  • discuss, scope and quote a project you've asked about
  • carry out and deliver a project if we work together
  • keep the business records the law requires
  • keep the site secure and working

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for marketing or share it with anyone for theirs. We don't make decisions about you by automated means.

5. Consent

We collect and use your information with your consent. By sending the contact form you consent to our using what you send for the purposes above.

You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to us. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect what was done before you withdrew it.

6. Cookies and embedded video

This site sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser.

The videos shown here are hosted by YouTube and Instagram and play through their players. When a player loads on the page, those companies may set their own cookies and collect information such as your IP address and viewing activity, under their own privacy policies. We don't receive that information.

You can block third-party cookies in your browser settings. Every video also has a link that opens it directly on YouTube or Instagram.

7. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who need it to provide their service to us:

  • our web host, which stores the site and its server logs
  • our form delivery service and our email provider, through which contact form messages reach us
  • Google Fonts, which serves the site's typefaces and receives your IP address when a page loads
  • YouTube and Instagram, whose players load when a video plays on the page

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

8. Transfers outside Québec

Some of these providers store or process information outside Québec, including in the United States. Information held there is subject to the laws of that place. We use established providers and limit what we send them to what the service needs.

9. How long we keep it

We keep a message for as long as we need it to answer you and, where a project follows, for the life of that project and for as long afterward as we need for our business and tax records. After that we delete it or make it anonymous. You can ask us to delete your message sooner.

10. How we protect it

The site is served over an encrypted connection. Access to messages is limited to the people at Patience Told who need them, on password-protected accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you send us.

11. Your rights

Under Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector and Canada's federal privacy law, you can:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you and get a copy of it
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date
  • withdraw your consent to our using it
  • ask us to delete it, or to stop sharing it, where the law provides for that
  • ask for information you gave us in a structured, commonly used format

To use any of these rights, write to karim@patiencetold.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We answer within 30 days.

12. Minors

This site isn't directed at children under 14, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you're under 14, ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

13. Privacy incidents

If personal information we hold is accessed, used or lost without authorisation and this creates a risk of serious harm, we will notify the people affected and the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, and we keep a record of every incident.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or the law changes. The dates at the top show when it last changed. If a change is significant, we'll flag it on the site.

15. Contact and complaints

Questions, requests and complaints about privacy go to Karim Elnaggar at karim@patiencetold.com.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca) or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).
